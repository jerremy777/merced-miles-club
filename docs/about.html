About

Merced Miles Club is a group of runners who enjoy running around Lake Merced in San Francisco in the counter-clockwise direction.

The club was founded in 2025 by a group of friends who wanted to create a fun and informal running challenge for themselves and others. The 100 Laps Challenge was born out of this desire to set a goal and celebrate the achievement of running 100 laps around the lake.

The club does not organize group runs or maintain schedules, but sometimes we'll be out there running around the lake. If you see us, say hi! We love meeting other members of the club.
