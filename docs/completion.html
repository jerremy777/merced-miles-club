Completion

After all laps on a 100 Laps Challenge Card have been recorded, the card may be submitted for a reward and recognition on the Finishers page.

Completed cards may be mailed to the following address:

Merced Miles Club
PO Box 5105
South San Francisco, CA 94080

A successful submission includes a card with all 100 squares punched with a push pin. Please also include the date of your first lap and the date of your 100th lap, as well as any message you'd like to share about your experience with the challenge.

When we receive your card, we will verify that all 100 squares have been punched and update the Finishers page with your name and completion details, and send your card back to you with a special sticker and a note of congratulations.

Rewards are sent on a rolling basis and may take some time to arrive after submission.
